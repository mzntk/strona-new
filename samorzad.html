Student Activities

As the President of the Student Council at the Faculty of MFiI UG, I was the organizer and coordinator of many educational and integration projects.

aMFIteatr of Science 1.0 with Prof. Andrzej Dragan

View event page

The inaugural edition of the flagship science popularization series at the Faculty. The main highlight was an open lecture by a prominent physicist. The initiative attracted science enthusiasts and successfully promoted an innovative approach to STEM fields.

aMFIteatr of Science 2.0 with Dr. Tomasz Miller

View event page

The second installment of the series featuring Dr. Tomasz Miller, who introduced complex concepts from the intersection of mathematics and physics. The event inspired students to independently explore topics outside the standard curriculum.

Gamer's Night and League of Legends Tournament

View event page

A modern event combining esports with networking, in collaboration with Kinguin Esports Lounge in Gdańsk. The LoL tournament brought together players from across the faculty, creating a space for integration around a passion for gaming.

aMFIteatr of Science 3.0 – Q-Con side event

View event page

A unique edition, organized as an accompanying event to the Q-Con international quantum technology conference. An educational mini-conference for high school students promoting the STEM path.

Pre-exam Mentoring and Night Studying

A comprehensive educational support program. The initiative combined substantive help from senior students with access to faculty infrastructure until late evening hours to reduce pre-exam stress.

MFIkołajki in Ygrek and Faculty Day

Integration events and the organization of the most important day for the WMFiI community. They included comprehensive communication and logistical support, building a friendly environment and strong faculty identity.
